Orthogroups

Orthogroups capture sets of genes that likely descend from a single ancestral gene. Use this page to scan the catalog, filter by orthogroup ID, and open a family for its detailed gene tree view.

Each entry links to a dedicated orthogroup page where you can inspect the tree and adjust the layout controls to make large families readable.

Tips for navigating

  • Search by exact orthogroup ID to jump to a family quickly.
  • Increase rows per page when you are scanning broadly.
  • Open the orthogroup page to see gene-level details.
